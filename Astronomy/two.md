DAY 002 — THE UNIVERSE AND ITS MYSTERIES
---------- What is the Universe? -------------------------------------------------

The Universe is everything. Simple: from the table in your room to exoplanets and dark matter in space.
The totality of everything is the Universe.

------------- What is the difference between a star, a planet, and a galaxy? --------------------------------
A galaxy contains many stars and planets, but a planet or a star cannot contain an entire galaxy.
A planet orbits a star, but a star doesn't usually orbit a planet.

------------ What is something about the Universe that you don't understand yet? ------------------------------
Dark matter: How did it originate? Is it dangerous by itself, or could it be dangerous in certain circumstances?
What is it made of? Why aren't scientists creating something that can help us study dark matter?
How can we study something if we don't know what it is?
